# Daily Data Center Account Brief

A daily news brief for a Texas Instruments account manager who covers data center and server business at four accounts.

- **Live page:** https://claude.ai/artifact/ABfSU4sGCFSgaB73yrXBk3
- **Page source:** `daily-brief.html`
- **Archive:** `briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.html` (one snapshot per day)

## Accounts

| Account | Focus |
|---|---|
| Microsoft (MSFT) | Azure data center builds, capex, Maia / Cobalt custom silicon, power and site deals |
| Dell Technologies (DELL) | PowerEdge / AI Factory servers, liquid cooling, AI server backlog and margins |
| HPE | ProLiant / Cray / XD servers, Juniper data center networking, AI system orders |
| AMD | EPYC, Instinct, Helios rack-scale systems, hyperscaler deals, data center revenue |

## Sections on the page

1. **Today in 60 seconds:** the five most important items across all accounts and the industry.
2. **Your accounts:** 3 to 4 items per account. Each item has a signal (Opportunity, Watch or Risk), a date, a short summary and source links. Each account ends with TI talking points: power (multiphase, 48 V, hot-swap/eFuse, sequencing), sensing, motor drivers for liquid cooling, interface and supply assurance. They're conversation starters, not claims about the customer.
3. **Global data center desk:** major worldwide news, grouped as United States, Europe, and Asia-Pacific & Middle East. Covers power, permitting, financing, big campuses and supply chain.
4. **Coming up:** earnings dates, trade shows and platform ramps. Unconfirmed dates are marked.

## Rules for each refresh

- Prefer news from the last 24 to 72 hours. Older items stay only if nothing newer replaces them.
- Every item links its source. Figures that only one secondary outlet reports are marked unconfirmed.
- Don't invent customer requirements or TI design wins.
