Today in 60 seconds

  1. Microsoft's AI spend holds; delivery is the bottleneck. FQ1 capex came in at $45.2B against roughly $50.7B expected because partners delivered capacity late. Azure grew 48%. FY27 capex is still about $170B.
  2. AMD and Meta went deeper on Oct 27. Meta has Helios racks in testing, and the deal covers a future EPYC called "Verano". AMD Q3 earnings are expected around Nov 3 (not yet confirmed).
  3. Dell has three new PowerEdge platforms ramping into Q1 CY27. R8822, M9882 and XE9810 use NVIDIA Vera CPUs and Groq 3 LPUs. Boards still being finalized are design-in openings.
  4. HPE's ProLiant Gen13 runs on 6th Gen EPYC and OCP racks. The XD245 (liquid-cooled) and XD285 ship in 2027. HPE also has a $1.2B Vultr order for AMD Helios racks.
  5. Power and permitting are the industry-wide constraint. 41 local ballot measures across 10 US states vote on data center limits. The UK is tying grid priority to a 6 GW target. Equipment lead times keep stretching.

  • OpportunityOct 28

    Capex came in under the street because AI cloud partners delivered capacity late. Next quarter's Azure guide is 49–50%. Microsoft added 24 data centers on five continents this quarter, and about 75% of capex now goes to short-lived assets (GPUs and CPUs).

TI talking points
  • Demand is real and capacity is late. Lead with supply assurance and lead-time commitments on power and analog.

  • 788 MW sites and grid limits keep 48 V rack power and power-shelf efficiency on the agenda.

    HyperFRAME Research · Channel Post MEA

HPE

HPE · ProLiant / Juniper
  • OpportunityOct 7

    First four ProLiant Gen13 servers, all on 6th Gen AMD EPYC

    DL525 (later 2026). DL585a, with up to 8 double-wide GPUs (early 2027). XD245, a 1OU 4-node direct liquid-cooled server, and XD285, a 2OU 2-node air-cooled server, both OCP-compliant (2027). HPE also added automatic self-encrypting drives and broader post-quantum crypto.

    HPE Newsroom · DCD · The Next Platform
  • OpportunityEarly Oct

    $1.2B Vultr order for AMD Helios racks with Juniper networking

    Oracle is also using Juniper as the network backbone for a gigawatt-scale AI build.

    Yahoo Finance
  • WatchEarly Oct

    Networking Investor Day: Juniper synergy target raised to $800M

    Networking is guided to a high-teens CAGR from FY26 to FY29, and data center networking is its fastest-growing market. Daiwa upgraded the stock to Outperform. The stock hit a record high after the Gen13 launch.

    datacenter.news · FMP
TI talking points
  • XD245 and XD285 are OCP Open Rack designs. Lead with 48 V bus conversion, hot-swap and rack power monitoring.
  • "$800M in synergies" means consolidating Juniper and Aruba platforms. Pitch common power and interface building blocks across the switch lines.
  • DL585a holds 8 GPUs per node, so it needs high-current rails and good telemetry.
